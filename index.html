What do the labels mean? (2023 & 2024 naming)
2023 / 2024Data-taking year. Both use the same tag-and-probe method; curves are directly comparable.
seed / unseedPhoton leg. seed = the seeded (lead) leg (probe ET>30); unseed = the unseeded (sublead) leg (probe ET>18, requires the tag to have fired the seeded leg).
EB / EEECAL region. EB = barrel (|η|<1.44); EE = endcap (1.57<|η|<2.5).
R9 …Shower-shape category. R9 = E3×3/ERAW (energy in the 3×3 crystals around the seed ÷ raw supercluster energy). Higher R9 = more contained shower. EB splits at 0.50–0.55, 0.55–0.85, >0.85; EE at 0.90–0.93, >0.93.

…_fullPath2024 — trigger selection. Adds the full HLT-path requirement (probe_passFullPath == 1) on top of the standard (SS) selection. Without the suffix = standard selection only.
preBpix / postBpix2023 — the two data-taking sub-eras, split at the BPix pixel-detector boundary.
