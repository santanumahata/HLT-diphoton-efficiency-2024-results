What do the labels mean? (2023 & 2024 naming)
2023 / 2024Data-taking year. All use the same tag-and-probe method; curves are directly comparable.
seed / unseedPhoton leg. seed = the seeded (lead) leg (probe ET>30); unseed = the unseeded (sublead) leg (probe ET>18, requires the tag to have fired the seeded leg).
EB / EEECAL region. EB = barrel (|η|<1.44); EE = endcap (1.57<|η|<2.5).
R9 …Shower-shape category. R9 = E3×3/ERAW (energy in the 3×3 crystals around the seed ÷ raw supercluster energy). Higher R9 = more contained shower.
without NF
with NF		2024 — η–R9 reweighting map: without vs with the normalising-flow (NF) correction. Same data, settings and fit; only the η–R9 weight map differs.
preBpix / postBpixData-taking sub-eras. 2023 splits at the BPix pixel-detector boundary (preBpix/postBpix).
bwDSCB⊗CMS
bwDSCB⊗Exp		Fit model (toggle below the plot). Signal = Breit–Wigner ⊗ double-sided Crystal Ball in both. Background = RooCMSShape (CMS, the nominal) or Exponential (Exp, the alternate). Efficiency = fitted pass fraction.
ErrorsError bars (toggle below the plot). stat ⊕ syst (default): fit uncertainty of the shown model ⊕ background-model systematic, syst = |eff(bwDSCB⊗CMS) − eff(bwDSCB⊗Exp)| per bin, upper error capped at 1 − eff (the bkg_syst / total_unc definition of the 2023 CSV). stat only: fit uncertainty as written by the fitter. The ratio pad combines the two curves' errors in quadrature.
